The problem / beyond another alert

A flag isn't a correction.
A recommendation isn't evidence.

Missing punches leave earnings unknown. Policy interpretations are hard to inspect. A conversational change can conceal arithmetic, provenance, or authority.

  • Managers need a safe path from evidence to a persisted decision.
  • Employees need to understand earnings and challenge an incorrect line.
  • Detection and manager approvals already exist; we do not claim those as novelty.

Our approach / inspectable trust

Every change carries
its reason and its reproduction.

  • Visible, versioned policy clauses → bounded compiled rules → human signature.
  • Full-period Decimal recomputation → before/after derivations → independent approval.
  • Authenticated employee confirmation for unknown or reduced paid time.
  • Line-by-line paycheck exploration and a dispute lane that never silently edits pay.

Hourly non-exempt, salaried non-exempt, and salaried exempt. No surveillance data.
